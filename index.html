THE PROBLEM

Strategy isn't always the problem.
Discipline is.

Revenge trading, overtrading, increasing risk and ignoring limits can undo a good plan. FOUR is built around the rules you set before you trade.

01 — HARD LOCK

Stop at your limit

Configure a daily loss or profit threshold. When the configured trigger is reached, the EA can block new entries according to its supported settings.

02 — COOLDOWN

Give yourself time

Pause trading after a trade so you can avoid impulsive re-entry. Cooldown behavior depends on the EA configuration.

03 — RISK LOCK

Keep risk consistent

Use pre-defined risk rules instead of increasing position size emotionally after a loss.

04 — ONE AT A TIME

Control exposure

Use configured restrictions to prevent unwanted simultaneous positions or trade stacking.
